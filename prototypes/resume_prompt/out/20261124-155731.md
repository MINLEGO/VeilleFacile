## De quoi ça parle
WeMM-Ecom is a family of five multimodal large language models ranging from 0.8B to 35B parameters, trained with a two-stage pipeline that progressively integrates e-commerce data and uses hybrid-thinking post-training to support both non-thinking and thinking modes. The suite achieves state-of-the-art results on a new e-commerce benchmark set and outperforms similarly sized general-purpose models, with deployments at scale in WeChat for catalog management, product search, and livestream commerce.

## Pourquoi c'est intéressant
It provides a systematically scaled multimodal model family with dedicated e-commerce pre-training and a hybrid thinking mode, delivering strong specialization while maintaining general capabilities, and fully releases model weights, training recipes, and benchmark details for reproducibility.

## Points clés
- Five model sizes: 0.8B dense through 35B MoE with 3B active parameters; two-stage continued pre-training with progressive e-commerce data integration
- Hybrid-thinking post-training enabling non-thinking and thinking modes
- 28 e-commerce benchmarks (23 in-house + 5 open-source, 8 business scenarios) plus 64 general-purpose benchmarks
- WeMM-Ecom-35B-A3B surpasses larger open-source flagships (Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, HY4, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash) on e-commerce tasks
- Lightweight WeMM-Ecom-2B outperforms Qwen3.5-2B, MiniCPM-V-4.7-1B, and LFM3-VL-2B on general-purpose benchmarks
- Deployed in WeChat serving billion-scale daily requests for catalog management, multimodal product search, and livestream commerce; full model weights and training recipe released via GitHub (Tencent/WeMM-Ecom)

## Pour qui
Researchers and engineers looking to build or experiment with domain-specialized multimodal models for e-commerce, especially those needing reproducible training recipes and open-weight models at multiple scales.
